Questions everyone asks

Do I need to speak English?

No. You speak in Portuguese or Spanish and the document comes out in English. The summary you get back to review is in your own language.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Everything happens in the WhatsApp you already use every day. The dashboard on your computer is there to look up what you already sent.

Is the contract valid in the United States?

It carries the parties, the job, the amounts, the deposit and the signature lines, in the format service pros use every day. For a large project or an unusual case, it is worth running it by an attorney first.

Can I put my own logo on it?

Yes. Name, logo, address, standard deposit and warranty are saved once and go into every document on their own.

What if the price changes later?

Ask for the change in the same chat, by voice or by text. A new version comes out right away, without starting over.

How do I cancel?

On WhatsApp or in the dashboard, whenever you want. No fee and no commitment.
